About QIZE

Core expertise. Integrated sourcing.

A modern entry-door hardware brand and supply-chain service provider for manufacturers, distributors, importers, project buyers and private-label brands worldwide.

QIZE is a modern entry-door hardware brand and supply-chain service provider, serving door manufacturers, distributors, importers, project buyers and private-label brands worldwide.

QIZE grew out of close, long-term collaboration with Hengtai Intelligent and continues to work closely with its team in product development, manufacturing and supply coordination. Drawing on accumulated industry experience, technical expertise and manufacturing resources, QIZE focuses its core product portfolio on entry-door pull handles, decorative door panels and illuminated door hardware systems.

From standard and illuminated pull handles to decorative panels, illuminated door numbers, frame lighting and other entrance-lighting components, we coordinate appearance, structure, lighting performance and installation requirements. Rather than treating each item as an isolated component, QIZE develops products as part of a coordinated entrance-door system.

Alongside our core product development and manufacturing collaboration, QIZE works with a qualified industry supply network to provide compatible smart locks, hinges, door closers, floor springs, automatic door operators, lock bodies and related components. Based on each customer’s door type, dimensions, functional requirements, target market and purchasing list, we support product matching, specification confirmation, quality inspection, consolidated sourcing, packaging coordination and export delivery.

Our objective is not simply to offer more products. We build professional expertise around our core categories and use supply-chain collaboration to complete the broader door hardware package. OEM and ODM support is available for dimensions, surface finishes, lighting configurations, branding, packaging and product documentation.

From a single entry-door pull handle to a coordinated hardware package, QIZE aims to be a reliable long-term partner with a practical understanding of entrance products, manufacturing and international sourcing.
